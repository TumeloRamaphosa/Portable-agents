# Operating cadence

Times below are in Africa/Johannesburg.

Nozi-Corp-Coffee runs a short, weekday operating rhythm. Each report covers only what is new. If nothing changed, nothing is posted.

## Weekdays

| Time | Check | Focus |
|------|-------|--------|
| 07:30 | Morning brief | What changed overnight, blockers, and anything that needs a human yes before the day runs on |
| 12:30 | Storefront and research check | Public storefront health, research inputs, and the competitor-digest pass described in [competitor-digest-method.md](competitor-digest-method.md) |
| 15:30 | Approvals and spend | Walk the approval queue. Flag money, contracts, paid usage, and customer-facing drafts. Do not approve or send them |
| 18:00 | Daily wrap | What landed, what is still open, and the next day's top items. Wraps are saved privately; only a short new-info note is shared when something changed |

## Monday

| Time | Check | Focus |
|------|-------|--------|
| 08:00 | Weekly review | Last week's crew output, open decisions, and the week ahead. Used to roll up research and launch readiness |

## Standing rules for every check

- Agents draft. Humans approve money, contracts, and customers.
- Do not spend, book, or start paid usage without a human yes.
- Do not send mail or messages without an approved draft or an explicit instruction from the owner.
- Do not write secrets or tokens into chat, the queue, or this folder. Name the key and its location only, in a private channel.
- Keep routine notes short. Silence is correct when nothing is new.
