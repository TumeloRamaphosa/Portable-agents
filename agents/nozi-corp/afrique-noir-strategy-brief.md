**Status:** Recommendations only. **Agent Chief Lord** approves money, contracts, customers, and outreach.

## Bottom line (three businesses)

1. **Storefronts** (Cafe Noir / studex-coffee, Nozi) — brand, retail, subscriptions, B2B café/office.
2. **Distribution** — wholesale, private label, roasting-as-a-service, logistics markup (steady volume).
3. **Raw green-coffee marketplace** — cooperatives (Rwanda, Uganda, Ethiopia) ↔ roasters/importers; **Execution Exchange / Studex Global Markets** settles traceable trades.

Coffee is the first commodity to **stream** (priced lots + documentary settlement) because benchmarks (ICE), standard lots, and traceability demand already exist.

## Hard constraints (agents must not contradict)

| Constraint | Implication |
|------------|-------------|
| **SA exchange control (draft crypto manual, Aug 2026)** | SA **entities** do not externalise crypto; individuals only via licensed CASPs. Import FX via authorised dealers. Stablecoin settlement = **offshore entity or licensed partners**, not an SA company wallet. |
| **Phase 3 commodities** | **ON HOLD** until independent sanctions/compliance sign-off. No data, wallet, or contract links to held lanes until cleared. Default **NO-GO**. |
| **Agent governance** | Nozi Chief / Nozi-Corp-Coffee **do not** execute spend, contracts, or customer contact. Queue → Tumelo. |

## Model recommendation (memo)

- **Marketplace:** agent/broker (Algrano-style)—fees on matchmaking + services, not principal inventory.
- **Afrique Noir distribution** = **anchor buyer** on the marketplace (cold-start for cooperatives).
- **Settlement (later phases):** licensed on-ramp → escrow (e.g. USDC where MiCA-compliant) → DvP on export/QC/EUDR docs → cooperative off-ramp. Utility token = **closed-loop fee credit only** until legal opinion per jurisdiction.
