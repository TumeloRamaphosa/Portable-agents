# Competitor digest method

A regular pass over public specialty-coffee storefronts, used to brief the owner. This folder records the method only. It does not record prices, quotes, or a named competitor list.

## What is scanned

From information that is already public on a storefront or product page:

- whether the storefront is reachable and what it offers
- pack sizes and product formats on display
- origin or farm claims, where shown
- roast style and product positioning
- retail or channel presence that the brand states publicly
- notable packaging or launch activity

Prices and other commercial figures are noted only in the private digest write-up, never in this folder and never in a public branch of this repository.

## Cadence

- **Weekday, 12:30.** A short storefront and research check. If nothing material changed, no digest is filed.
- **As needed, same day.** If a public change looks launch-relevant, a digest draft is prepared the same day.
- **Monday, 08:00.** A weekly roll-up of what was scanned, with only material changes carried forward.

Digests that contain figures or source detail are filed as drafts on the [studex-coffee](https://github.com/TumeloRamaphosa/studex-coffee) repository, not here. Those drafts stay unmerged until the owner says otherwise.

## How a digest is filed

1. Scan only public pages.
2. Write a short draft: what changed, why it might matter, and whether any owner decision follows.
3. Leave prices and supplier commentary out of any public summary.
4. Do not reach out to a competitor or a supplier from a digest. Outreach, if any, is a separate approval-queue item.
