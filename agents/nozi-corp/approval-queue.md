# Approval queue

This is the process other agents should follow. It does not include live queue items or their contents.

## Purpose

The queue is the single place where work that needs a human yes is collected. Nozi-Corp-Coffee keeps it moving. Agent Chief Lord is the approver for money, contracts, and customer-facing work.

## How an item flows

1. **Draft.** An agent files the item as a draft: what it is, why it is needed, and what decision is requested. The item is not sent, paid, booked, printed, or published.
2. **Waiting for approval.** The item is ready for the owner to review. Agents may answer questions and revise the draft. They do not change it to approved, and they do not carry it out.
3. **Approved.** Only the human owner can mark an item approved and authorise the action.
4. **On hold.** The item stays in the queue but is not moving, for example because a required input is missing or a wider decision is still open.
5. **Declined or withdrawn.** The item is closed without action.

## What must go through the queue

- spend, invoices, bookings, and paid usage
- contracts and commercial terms
- customer or buyer outreach, and any message that would go to a customer
- public announcements, packaging that will be printed, and storefront or campaign publishing
- anything that would create or change a credential, or use a paid API

Ordinary research notes and internal checklists can be drafted in the usual way. If they later need a spend or a public action, they enter the queue at that point.

## Draft-only rule

Agents never:

- approve their own drafts, or anyone else's
- send a message, mail, or payment from a draft
- change an item's status to approved
- treat silence as approval

A draft stays a draft until the owner approves it, or until they give an explicit instruction to carry out that specific action.

## What does not belong in a queue item

- secrets, tokens, passwords, or full credential values
- private addresses, phone numbers, or identity documents
- raw customer lists

If a draft needs a credential, refer to it by key name and private location only.
