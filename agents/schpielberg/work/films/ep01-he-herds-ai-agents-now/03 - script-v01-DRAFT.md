## Long-form master (16:9) — seven chapters

### CH1 — DAWN · shot H01-01
*(herd cresting the rise, gold horizon)*
[[
]]Before the sun is up, the herd is already moving.this an ankole cow tyingg into the orignial hypthesis of "cattle of kings"

Nobody tells them where the grass is. Nobody draws them a map. They walk, and the land answers.

For as long as anyone can remember, that was the work. Read the sky. Read the ground. Know your animals by their walk. Make a hundred decisions before breakfast, and carry every one of them alone.

*(pause — dust in the light)*

He still wakes at the same hour. But the herd looks different now.

### CH2 — THE HAND · shot H01-02
*(elder's hand on fence post, herd soft in the distance)*

This hand built fences. Fixed engines. Signed papers it barely had time to read.

A founder's hands are never idle, because a company is a herd that never stops moving. Customers drift. Money strays. The work multiplies in the dark.

The old way was to hold tighter. More hours. More tabs open. More things only he could do.

The new way is older than it looks.

### CH3 — THE FOOTPRINTS · shot H01-03
*(adult and child prints walking beside hoof prints)*

Every herder learns the same way: walking beside someone who already knows.

Not by being replaced. By being accompanied.

That is the whole idea. You do not hand over the herd. You teach someone to walk it with you — bounded, watched, accountable. Step by step, until the steps become a system.

### CH4 — THE WIRE · shot H01-04
*(fence wire catching the light, land beyond)*

A fence is not a wall. It is a decision about where things should go.

So the first thing the machine is given is not freedom. It is a brief. A boundary. This far, no further. These tasks, not those. Every action inside the fence is logged, sourced, and shown.

Nothing leaves the fence without a yes.

### CH5 — THE LEARNING · shot H01-05
*(gold chain of links across the sky)*

Each task teaches the next. Research becomes evidence. Evidence becomes a draft. A draft becomes a decision waiting for him.

The links hold because each one is checked. Sources cited. Uncertainty marked in plain language. A claim that cannot be proven is labelled a claim.

This is not magic. It is discipline, repeated at machine speed.

### CH6 — THE GATES · shots H01-06 → H01-07
*(herd through the timber gate; four gates of gold light resolve ahead)*

In the evening the herd comes through the gate — because the gate was opened.

Four gates stand between an idea and the world. Research. Draft. Review. Release. Every one of them opens on a signal from one person.

The agents walk. The founder decides.

He reads the evidence. He asks the hard question. He says yes, or he says no, and the system honours both the same way.

### CH7 — THE TABLE · shot H01-08
*(empty table, lamps lit, chairs pulled out)*

The point was never the machines.

The point is this table. The hour he gets back. The decisions made with evidence instead of exhaustion.

He herds AI agents now. They move. He decides.

**StudBot.** Agents that prepare. Founders that decide.

Start a 30-day guided agent pilot.
