## Story beats (approved structure from [[09 - Video Production Plan - Open Notebook NotebookLM Higgsfield]])

1. A founder has too many disconnected tasks.
2. StudBot receives a bounded brief.
3. Agents research and prepare work.
4. The system surfaces evidence and asks for a decision.
5. The founder approves the next action.
6. The OUTBOX contains a real deliverable.
7. End on the one CTA.

## The creative parallel

A herder moves cattle with patience and judgment — the animals walk, the herder decides. StudBot is the same shape: agents do the walking, the founder makes every decision. The episode never shows a robot, a screen, or a face. It shows land, cattle, gates, and light.
