# Studex Arcade Cup — Vision Film Script v0.2

**INTERNAL DRAFT v0.2 · 18 Oct 2026 (SAST)** · Prepared for Agent Chief Lord (Tumelo Ramaphosa) and Schpielberg (production). Not for external sharing. Base: Ledger's Script v0.1 (S01–S22). Source of truth: Agentic Rise Programme Plan v0.2 (internal). Founder story from MY Story/STORY-AND-DIRECTION and Agent Chief Lord's origin notes of 18 Oct only.

- **Logline:** A grandfather teaches a boy to see the value in a herd. Ten years after he looked past the fence, that boy, Tumelo Ramaphosa, herds AI agents instead of cattle, and Studex is building the Studex Arcade Cup to give Africa's next generation the global stage his family hoped for.
- **Runtime:** 14:00 (22 scenes, S01–S22; timecodes unchanged from v0.1).
- **VO:** 1802 words (tags and [TBC] notes excluded), about 129 words per minute against a 130 wpm target.
- **Narrator:** Studbot, the Studex robot, labelled as AI on screen. It speaks about Tumelo in the third person and never claims to be him or to have lived his story.
- **Through-line:** grandfather → herd → "looking past the fence" → learning (blockchain, cloud, data science) → the digital herder (from herding cattle to herding AI agents) → a global stage for the next generation.
- **Brand (17 Oct brand note, applied 18 Oct):** ground #050503, single accent #C5A163. Archivo Black for headlines and titles; IBM Plex Mono for straps, captions, dates and metadata. No other palette or typefaces. Ankole horn, gold horizon line and four-gate motifs appear only as generated environment elements in this palette, never as logo marks or overlay graphics. Never generate StudBot, Naledi, the founder, packaging or certification marks: Studbot is voice only, unless an existing approved asset is supplied. Voice: confident, not hyped. Say "Africa is being built", never "rising". No "revolutionary" or "world-class".
- **Conventions:** [TARGET] marks every number and date in VO and on-screen text. It is a production tag, so don't speak it or show it. Every quantity is phrased as an aim. Numerals inside product names (CS2) are not figures. [TBC] means Agent Chief Lord must supply or approve the item before recording. **[REAL CLIP SLOT]** means real footage of Tumelo, his family or the business should go here. **[REAL ASSET SLOT]** means a real Studex screen, design or file should go here. Generated visuals are the fallback for every slot.
- **Hard rules:** On-screen and spoken name is "Studex Arcade Cup" only. No government, sponsor or partner names or logos. No identifiable real minors: school scenes use generated players only. No keys, IPs, emails, hostnames or internal paths on any screen. No live drone flights: simulation only.
