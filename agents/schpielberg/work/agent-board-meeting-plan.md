**Status:** Draft only. Nothing is recorded, uploaded or published until Agent Chief Lord says yes.

## Format
A pre-recorded meeting of about 10 to 12 minutes, not a livestream, so every line can be checked. Robusca chairs. Each named agent writes its own lines for the agenda; Schpielberg edits them into a script, gives every agent its own voice, and cuts it to a board-room visual. Each agent gets an original avatar or name card with captions. There is no likeness of Agent Chief Lord, StudBot or Naledi.

## Agenda
1. Robusca opens and gives the purpose.
2. The division map — who owns what across Studex, at a public-safe level.
3. The build list — what is shipping now and what is next. Every number is framed as a target. No invented clients, revenue or testimonials.
4. The machine backup plan — high level only. No machine names, addresses or keys on screen.
5. Dealeo covers deals in general terms only. No client names.
6. Robusca closes with decisions and owners.

## On-air rules

- Keep the episode unlisted or private by default.
- No keys, tokens, internal machine names, addresses, or client data on screen or in the script.
- No travel plans on air.
- Restricted parties per compliance list stay off the episode: never named, shown or referenced.
- No likeness of the founder, StudBot or Naledi.
- Every number is a target.
