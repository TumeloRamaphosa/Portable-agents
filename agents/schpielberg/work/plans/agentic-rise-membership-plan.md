## 2. Positioning

**One line (public):** *Agentic Rise is the build room for African founders who want AI agents working with them, not instead of them.*

**Positioning statement (internal):** For founders and small teams in Southern and East Africa who are curious about AI agents but afraid of losing control, or of paying in dollars for tools they cannot run, Agentic Rise is a paid build community. You learn to run a small herd of agents (one shared memory, four human gates) by watching a real African company, Studex, do it on its own desks every week. Most AI-automation communities are built for US agency owners. Agentic Rise is built around African time zones, rand-friendly pricing, and a rule that nothing leaves the gate without a human yes.

**The story spine (Studbot narrates in the third person):** He learned cattle from his grandfather and wanted their work and their animals valued far beyond the fence. Studex is ten years [TARGET: verify framing] into that search. Today he is a **digital herder**: the agents carry the daily work and bring the reports home, and he still decides.

**Differentiators to say on camera (all true today):**
1. **Human gates first.** The four-gates rule is the product, not a disclaimer.
2. **Real desks, real tasks.** Build-with-me episodes are screen recordings of Studex's own agent work (Meat first), with sensitive details redacted.
3. **African clock and wallet.** Live builds run in SAST. Pricing is set below the US leaders (§4).
4. **A path, not a pitch.** Free, then paid, then StudBot Rise (done with you). Membership never buys selection, capital or introductions.

**Voice rules (from CC/10 and script v0.2):** confident, not hyped. Say "Africa is being built", never "Africa is rising". No "revolutionary" and no "world-class". Studbot always says "he" and never "I was". Every video is labelled AI or synthetic, with a "Narrated by Studbot (AI)" strap.

**Do not say:** income claims, member counts, "guaranteed" anything, partner or government names, any Russian-linked party, or the held names. Never show infrastructure detail (hostnames, ports, IPs, keys, emails, internal paths).

### 4.3 Why these numbers

1. **Below the leaders, not a discount brand.** $49 is about 56% of Jack's $87, 38% of Nate's $129 and 27% of Nick's $184. Those communities have large YouTube audiences and long track records. Agentic Rise starts with **zero members and zero testimonials**, so a price at parity would be hard to justify on day one.
2. **African purchasing power and FX.** A dollar subscription is a moving rand cost. Going under $50 keeps the monthly charge under about R800 at today's rate. The optional R649/R799 rail removes FX entirely for SA members (§6).
3. **A clean step to the top rung.** StudBot Rise at R4,599 is about 5.7× the R805 standard price. That gap is large enough that the paid rung feels like the obvious first step, and small enough that a member can see the upgrade path.
4. **The founding price rewards early risk.** The $39 price is locked while a member stays subscribed. It creates urgency without fake scarcity: the cap is real because it is set by you.
5. **Rejected alternatives:** $87 parity (no proof yet), $19 (too low to fund weekly live builds, and it signals low value), and a free trial on day one (it lifts sign-ups but invites churn. Jack runs a 7-day trial at scale. Revisit after day 90).

**Annual plan:** offer it from day one. It is the strongest retention lever available (corp-email-marketing segmentation: annual members get a separate onboarding track).
