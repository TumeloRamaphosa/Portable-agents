# Production Plan: STUDEX "The Digital Herder" + 9:16 cut-downs
Status: PLAN ONLY. No credits spent, nothing posted. Every paid call and every publish waits for Tumelo's (or Katlego's) explicit yes.

## Pipeline
1. **Script (Hermes + Obsidian):** Hermes drafts from the `studex-story` skill and story-draft.md into `MY Story/`. Naledi's desk checks the line. Claim-check table: every line is sourced or marked needs-review.
2. **Boards and drafts (local, free):** ComfyUI + Qwen on the local GPU box for animatics and look tests. Freepik for reference, stock and textures (licence checked).
3. **Hero shots (Higgsfield, ON HOLD):** only the 6–8 hero shots (dawn herd, gates of light, city plate push, agent rooms, Arcade world, the table). Preflight each with `get_cost: true`, keep to the 250/day cap, and generate nothing until the shot list and credit total are approved. On timeout, reuse the job_id. No faces, likeness, logos or text.
4. **3D/motion (Blender):** gold corridors, four gates and character turnarounds for the original cast.
5. **Edit/grade (DaVinci Resolve), utility (ffmpeg):** assembly, colour, sound mix, zoompan pushes (zero credits), 9:16 reframes, captions burn-in, exports.
6. **Distribution (Blotato):** load approved cuts as **scheduled drafts only** after sign-off. Never auto-publish.
