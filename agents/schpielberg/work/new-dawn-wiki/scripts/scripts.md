# Scripts
## Opening narration (Studbot)
> Before the sun is up, the herd is already moving. His grandfather knew the Ankole by their walk. In a time when families were kept from owning other kinds of wealth, the herd was the bank. Ten years ago, his grandson stood at that same fence and looked past it, toward markets far across the ocean. I am Studbot. I was not the farmer. I am one of the herd he walks with now.

## Treatment
**Logline:** A grandson of cattle herders becomes a digital herder. His agents walk the work and he makes the decisions, while Studex aims to open doors for African enterprise.
**Tone:** quiet, documentary, gold on obsidian. No faces, no fake dashboards, no generated text.
**Outline:** (1) Dawn herd and heritage; (2) a farmer who looks past the fence; (3) learning years from chain to cloud to data; (4) Global Markets city of corridors; (5) Super Agents rooms; (6) Agent OS fence and four gates; (7) StudBot in the Founders Den; (8) Agentic Rise seat; (9) Arcade and the Arcade Cup (aim); (10) the table, with time given back; (11) city plate and CTA.
**Visual system:** land, cattle, gates and light for the past; black-and-gold rooms for the present; neon for Arcade. Typography is added in post (Archivo Black / IBM Plex Mono).
**Sound:** wind, hooves and wire hum, moving into soft synth. One locked synthetic Studbot voice at about 90 wpm.
**End line:** "The agents walk. He decides." Start a 30-day guided agent pilot.
