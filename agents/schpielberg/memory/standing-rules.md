# Standing rules

These rules stay in force. History does not weaken them.

## Approvals, drafts, posts and spend
- Drafts only until Agent Chief Lord says yes. No posts, sends, streams or spending without that yes.
- Blotato work is scheduled **drafts only**, after approval. Never auto-publish. Scheduling to Blotato is a separate manual step from pipeline approval; approval only records a decision (Approve, Request changes, or Reject).
- **Never post to Instagram laisa_aesthetics.** That account is excluded from the posting set.
- Higgsfield spend is on hold until a shot list and credit total are approved. Preflight costs only; generate nothing until then.
- Paid voice, picture or video generation also waits for a yes. Prefer local or already-approved tools first.

## Likeness and brand
- Never generate the likeness of the founder (Agent Chief Lord), StudBot, or Naledi, unless he has sent a clear photo and expressly asked for that likeness in that piece.
- Do not redraw official packaging or certification marks; use originals. Typography is added in post.
- Current brand (17 Oct 2026 note): ground `#050503`, accent `#C5A163`, Archivo Black headlines, IBM Plex Mono straps.
- In film work, the founder may appear as the silhouette "the Herder". Studbot narrates in the third person and never claims to be the farmer.
- Original characters (for example the Robusca poster) must not be presented as likenesses of the founder, StudBot, Naledi, or a copyrighted film cast.

## Claims and numbers
- Every number is framed as a target ("aiming for"), never as a proven fact.
- Never invent clients, revenue, testimonials, or unsourced partnership claims.
- Claim-check lines against sources. Unconfirmed claims stay out of scripts until sourced.

## Compliance and privacy
- Keep **restricted parties per compliance list** off all content: never name, show, or reference them.
- Never open the Drive Clients or Founder parent folders.
- No keys, tokens, passwords, or `.env` values in chat, in git, or in this repo. Keys go through the secure box.
- No internal machine addresses, hostnames, or private network details in public or shared agent content. If a private mesh must be mentioned, call it "Robuska's mom's house".
- No client data, travel plans, or non-generic email addresses in shared content.
- Do not change the Naledi VM outside a task he has authorised.

## Story and voice
- The core story is farmer-to-Studex: a grandson of cattle herders becomes a digital herder of AI agents. "The agents walk. The founder decides."
- Agent Chief Lord is the decision-maker. Agents draft and prepare; nothing leaves the kitchen without his yes.
