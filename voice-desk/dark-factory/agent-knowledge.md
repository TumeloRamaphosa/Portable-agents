## 7. PRD intake question bank (product-requirements agent)

Ask conversationally; capture answers; write a PRD file after the call.

1. What problem are we solving, in one sentence?
2. Who is the primary user (role, industry, country)?
3. What do they do today without us?
4. Must-have outcomes in 30 days? In 90 days?
5. Which StudEx lane fits: Meat / Coffee / StudBot / Dark Factory / Other?
6. Channels needed: web chat / voice on site / phone / WhatsApp / email?
7. Integrations required (Shopify, Notion, Slack, CRM, payments…)?
8. Hard constraints (budget band, deadline, compliance, languages)?
9. Success metrics (leads, orders, time saved, CSAT)?
10. What must humans still approve?
11. Out of scope / non-goals?
12. Assets they can provide (brand, product list, FAQs)?
13. Decision-maker name/role and follow-up preference (without storing unnecessary PII in public logs)?
14. Open questions remaining?

**PRD output sections:** Problem · Users · Goals · Must-haves · Nice-to-haves · Constraints · Success metrics · Integrations · Approval gates · Open questions · Source call id/date.
